National Conference on Physics: 2025

27 February 2025

This is the first conference I have attended as a Presenter. I preseted my research poster on ion-ion interaction in Liquid metals. I was also a volunteer for this event, working for Bangladesh Physical Society.

I utilized the event to visit Rajshahi City knowing fully well that this may be the only time I will get such an opportunity. I saw the Rajshahi College, the University of Rajshahi, the New market. I also visited the silk mills. Unfortunately the Varendra Museum was closed during this time (Thursday, Friday). Because of time constraints I could not visit the Puthia Rajbari. I left for Dhaka on Friday night.
